## Lessons and next gates

Correct final answers can hide faulty steps, missing diagram interpretations or erroneous reviewer calculations. Numeric labels and signs must be checked independently. Explicit approximations must be distinguished from exact identities. A strict protocol rejected a reviewer calculator call; the recorded command accessed no project answers, and a fresh no-tools retry passed. Original failed attempts and normalized-output provenance were preserved privately.

Next: finish the remaining review groups; repair or replace unresolved cases within their curriculum cells; rebuild and re-review every affected linked exercise; rerun collective scientific, split, source, schema and processor checks; then freeze the training release and evaluation plan. The unchanged-base comparison and existing no-regression gates still control any later learning experiment. Neither review completion nor lower training loss guarantees better JEE solving.

Only aggregate documentation is published here. Private questions, answers, identifiers, screenshots, raw reviewer evidence and protected evaluation material are excluded. The private recovery record remains local, not an off-device backup.
