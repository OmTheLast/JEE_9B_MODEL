THE METHOD
What the harness does
A browser-side structure around the model, designed for readable work rather than a bare answer.
02 / 03
01 / READ
Frame the problem
Subject, exam, expected answer type and possible thermodynamics sign conventions become explicit before generation.
02 / DECOMPOSE
Choose a route
The prompt asks for givens, a short subgoal plan, essential calculations and all requested branches.
03 / INSPECT
Show the limits
The page checks whether a plan, check and final line exist. These are format checks, not proof that the mathematics is correct.
This first browser harness does not yet have an independent symbolic checker or trusted calculator. Those are research tasks, and the 9B MLX adapters cannot be loaded by Transformers.js as-is.
