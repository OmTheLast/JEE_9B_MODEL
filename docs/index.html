THE METHOD
What the harness does
A browser-side structure around the model, designed for readable work rather than a bare answer.
02 / 03
01 / READ
Frame the problem
Subject, exam, expected answer type and possible thermodynamics sign conventions become explicit before generation.
02 / DECOMPOSE
Choose a route
The prompt asks for givens, a short subgoal plan, essential calculations and all requested branches.
03 / INSPECT
Show the limits
The page checks requested sections and runs a separate calculation for one simple kinematics pattern. Other mathematical steps remain unverified.
This first browser harness does not yet have a general symbolic checker or calculator. Those are research tasks, and the 9B MLX adapters cannot be loaded by Transformers.js as-is.
